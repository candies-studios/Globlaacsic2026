Visiting India

Essential Information for International Delegates

Time Zone

India operates on Indian Standard Time (IST) at GMT +5:30 / UTC +5:30

There is no daylight saving time adjustment. A unified time zone applies nationwide.

Electrical System

Voltage: 220–240 volts at 50-cycle frequency

Appliances designed for 110V systems (US, Japan) need adapters. Hotels typically provide universal sockets.

Currency

The Indian Rupee (₹) is the official currency with common denominations of ₹500, ₹100, and ₹50 notes.

Major international credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) are widely accepted.

Weather (April)

April brings warm, dry conditions with temperatures between 25°C to 34°C (77°F to 93°F).

Pack lightweight clothing. Venues and hotels are air-conditioned.

Socket Types in India

  • Type C: Two-hole round pin sockets
  • Type D/M: Three-hole round pin sockets

Payment Methods

  • International credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express)
  • Digital payment options in urban areas
  • Currency exchange at airports, banks, and hotels

Travel Tips

  • Ensure your passport is valid for at least 6 months
  • Check visa requirements for your country
  • Consider travel insurance
  • Check with your bank about international transaction policies
  • Download offline maps of Mumbai
