On behalf of CGTMSE, the host institution, I am pleased to welcome you to the Global Symposium on Credit Guarantee & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026, to be held on April 23-24 in Mumbai. We are honored to bring together global policymakers, financial institutions, practitioners, and thought leaders on a common platform to engage in meaningful dialogue on the evolving role of credit guarantee mechanisms.

In a rapidly changing financial landscape, credit guarantees remain a critical tool for enhancing access to finance, strengthening risk-sharing frameworks, and supporting enterprise-led growth. As innovation continues to reshape financial systems, this symposium provides an important opportunity to exchange global perspectives, share best practices, and explore forward-looking solutions that can improve the effectiveness and impact of credit guarantee frameworks worldwide.

I am confident that the collective expertise of our participants will contribute to insightful discussions and constructive outcomes. Alongside the technical program, we hope you will also experience the dynamism and cultural richness of Mumbai, a city that reflects resilience, enterprise, and global connectivity.

Thank you for joining us and for your commitment to advancing inclusive and sustainable financial systems. I wish you a rewarding and productive symposium.

Shri Manish Sinha
Chief Executive Officer, CGTMSE
