Welcome Message

Message from the Chief Organizer

"We are honored to welcome you to the Global Symposium & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026 on Credit Guarantee. This prestigious gathering brings together global policymakers, financial institutions, practitioners, and thought leaders on a common platform to engage in meaningful dialogue on the evolving role of credit guarantee mechanisms."

Event Overview

The Global Symposium & 38th ACSIC Conference 2026 on Credit Guarantee will be held on April 23-24, 2026 at The Taj Mahal Palace, Mumbai.

Key Focus Areas

This international conference emphasizes credit guarantees as instrumental for:

  • Expanding access to financial services for underserved populations
  • Strengthening risk-sharing mechanisms between lenders and borrowers
  • Fostering enterprise-led economic development and innovation
  • Promoting financial inclusion and sustainable entrepreneurship
  • Exchanging best practices and policy innovations globally
